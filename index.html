Core Capabilities

01 · Identity

Cryptographic Identity

Decentralized identifiers (DIDs) anchored on Ethereum. Your identity is your keypair — no central authority, no single point of failure.

02 · Consent

Granular Permissions

Grant access by scope, by provider, by time window. Every consent is a smart contract — auditable, revocable, and enforceable on-chain.

03 · Revocation

Irreversible Revocation

Revoke a single consent or all at once. Once confirmed, the transaction is final. The service provider loses access immediately — no appeals, no delays.

04 · Providers

Provider Transparency

See every service provider connected to your data, their verification status, access history, and request volume. Full visibility, no black boxes.

How It Works

Three steps. On-chain. Final.

01

Create Your Vault

Generate a DID from your Ethereum address. Your keypair is your identity — no email, no password, no central database.

02

Grant Consent

Approve data access for specific providers, scoped to specific data types, with expiration dates. Each grant is a transaction.

03

Revoke Anytime

Revoke individual consents or all at once. The revocation is on-chain and immediate. Service providers lose access the moment you confirm.

Security Model

No trust required.

On-Chain Enforcement

Every consent and revocation is a smart contract transaction. No off-chain API can override what the chain enforces.

Zero-Knowledge Proofs

Prove you're over 18 without revealing your birthdate. Prove you're a citizen without revealing your ID number. ZKPs for selective disclosure.

Key Rotation

Rotate your signing key without losing your identity history. Recovery keys ensure you never lose access to your vault.
